Register for free — no credit card required
02
To start planning, create a free WedPlan Lanka account. Registration takes less than a minute. Click "Get Started Free" from the welcome page or "Create one free" from the login page.
🖼️
Screenshot: Register Page

Registration Form Fields

FieldDescription
Full Name *Your full name — e.g. Sanduni Perera
Email Address *A valid email address. Used to log in.
Phone Number *Your mobile number — e.g. +94 77 123 4567
Password *Choose a strong password (minimum 8 characters)
Confirm Password *Re-enter your password to confirm it matches

Steps to Register

1
Go to the Welcome Page

Click "Get Started Free" in the navigation bar or the hero section.

2
Fill in the Registration Form

Enter your full name, email, phone number, and a secure password.

3
Click "Create My Account"

If there are errors (e.g. email already taken), they appear in red below the field.

4
You're in!

You are automatically logged in and redirected to set up your wedding workspace.

⚠️

Email must be unique. If you see "The email has already been taken", use a different email or log in to your existing account.

Wedding Checklist
Track every task for your big day
06
The Checklist helps you track every task needed for your wedding. Tasks are grouped by category and come pre-loaded based on your wedding type. You can add, complete, prioritise, and delete tasks at any time.
🖼️
Screenshot: Wedding Checklist Page
Paste a screenshot of the checklist here

Adding a Task

1
Click "+ Add Task"

The add task form expands below the header.

2
Fill in the task details

Enter a title, select a category, set priority and an optional due date.

3
Click "Add Task"

The task appears in the list under its category.

Task Form Fields

FieldDescription
Task Title *A short description — e.g. "Book Poruwa decorator"
CategoryGroup the task — Venue, Photography, Catering, etc.
PriorityLow 🟢, Medium 🟡, High 🟠, or Urgent 🔴
Due DateOptional deadline for the task

Managing Tasks

☑️ Mark Complete

Click the checkbox on the left of any task to toggle it between Pending and Completed. Completed tasks show with a strikethrough.

🎯 Change Priority

Click the coloured priority badge on any task to open a dropdown. Select a new priority and click the green tick to save.

🗑️ Delete a Task

Click the ✕ button on the right of any task. A confirmation prompt will appear before deletion.

📊 Progress Bar

The progress bar at the top shows how many tasks are completed out of the total. This also feeds into your Dashboard progress.

💡

Tasks are grouped by category automatically. The category header shows how many tasks are completed in that group — e.g. "Venue (2/5)".

⚠️

Free plan limit: Up to 20 tasks. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited tasks.

Budget Tracker
Track every rupee of your wedding spend
07
The Budget Tracker lets you record estimated and actual costs for every wedding expense in LKR. See at a glance how much you've spent, how much is remaining, and where you're over or under budget.
🖼️
Screenshot: Budget Tracker Page
Paste a screenshot of the budget tracker here

Summary Cards

📋 Estimated Total

The sum of all estimated costs across your budget items.

💸 Actual Spent

The sum of all actual costs you have recorded so far.

✅ Remaining (green)

Estimated minus actual. Shown in green when under budget.

⚠️ Over Budget (red)

If actual exceeds estimated, remaining turns red with "(over)" label.

Adding a Budget Item

1
Click "+ Add Item"

The add form expands. (Button is hidden if you've reached the free plan limit.)

2
Fill in the details

Enter item name, category, estimated cost. Actual cost is optional — fill it in later once paid.

3
Click "Add Item"

The item appears in the table below.

Budget Item Fields

FieldDescription
Item Name *e.g. "Venue Booking", "Photographer"
Categorye.g. Venue, Catering, Photography, Decorations
Estimated Cost *Your expected cost in LKR
Actual CostWhat you actually paid. Leave blank until payment is made.
NotesOptional — e.g. "Deposit paid, balance due 2 weeks before"

Editing a Budget Item

Click the ✏️ edit icon on any row to expand an inline edit form. Update estimated cost, actual cost, or notes, then click Save.

⚠️

Free plan limit: Up to 5 budget items. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited items.

Adding a Note

Note Fields

FieldDescription
Title *A short heading for the note — e.g. "Photographer contact", "Venue deposit reminder"

Managing Notes

Click the pencil icon on any note card. The card switches to edit mode inline — update the title or content and click Save.

Changing Wedding Type

If you change your wedding type, a confirmation modal appears with two options:

🔄 Replace Default Tasks Only

Removes pre-built checklist tasks and adds new ones for the new wedding type. Your custom tasks are kept.

🗑️ Clear Entire Checklist

Removes ALL tasks including custom ones, then adds a fresh checklist for the new wedding type.

Support Access (Admin Toggle)

The Support Access section lets you control whether your wedding planner can view and edit your workspace on your behalf.

🔒 Disabled (Default)

Only you can access your workspace. Recommended for normal use.

🔓 Enabled

Your planner can view and edit your data. A warning banner appears at the top of every page. Last access time is shown.

⚠️

When admin access is enabled, a yellow warning banner appears at the top of every page. Toggle it off when you no longer need planner assistance.

How to Upgrade

1
Click "Upgrade to Pro" anywhere in the app

You'll find this button in the sidebar banner, on the welcome page pricing section, or in feature-locked banners throughout the app.

2
The Upgrade Modal Opens

Shows the Pro plan price and a WhatsApp contact button.

3
Click "Chat on WhatsApp"

Opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message to the WedPlan Lanka team requesting an upgrade.

4
Complete Payment

The team will guide you through payment. Once confirmed, your account is upgraded to Pro immediately.

Where to Find the Upgrade Button

  • Sidebar bottom banner (visible to all Free users)
  • Welcome page — Pricing section — Pro Plan card
  • Budget page — when the free limit is reached
  • Guest page — Bride/Groom list feature banner
  • Sidebar — locked Pro feature links (Agenda, Invitation, Seating)
💡

The Pro plan is a one-time payment per wedding. Once upgraded, you have lifetime access to all Pro features for your wedding workspace.
